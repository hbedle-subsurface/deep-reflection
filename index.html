Amplitude spectrum. Shaded wedges are removed.

      

      
Steep coherent events — migration artifacts, ground roll remnants, some multiples — live at high dip. Geologic reflectors on a 2D line are usually well under 0.1 samples per trace.

    

  


  

    
Structure-oriented smoothing

    
    

      

        
        
      

      

        
        
      

      

        
        
      

      

        
        
      

      
Edge protection holds the filter back where the local image is not linear — at faults, terminations and pinchouts. At 0 it smooths everywhere and will heal real discontinuities.

    

  


  

    
Detail boost

    
    

      

        
        
      

      

        
        
      

      
Large-scale amplitude is separated from fine structure and the fine part is rescaled. Above 1× this sharpens thin beds and low-contrast terminations. It will not help with a broad dim zone — that is a large-scale feature and lands in the base. Use AGC for those.

The removed panel 

      
The removed panel is the arithmetic difference between the input and the kept
      result, so the two panels sum to the input. Random noise appears there as speckle,
      coherent noise as organized events at a dip differing from the reflectors, and
      signal as reflector geometry.

      
Two cases where signal reaches the removed panel: structure-oriented smoothing
      with edge protection at 0 averages across a discontinuity narrower than the
      smoothing length, and an f-k dip limit set below the dip of the steepest
      reflectors rejects them along with the coherent noise.
