# Figures

The tool looks for two files here and hides the figure silently if either is
missing, so the site works whether or not they are present.

    WY01_map.png       location map of COCORP Wyoming Lines 1, 1A and 2
    WY01_section.png   the unmigrated section showing the Wind River thrust

Both are from Brewer et al. (1980), Tectonophysics 62, 165-189, © Elsevier. The
captions and credit lines are already written into `index.html`; the credit
currently reads "Reproduced pending permission". See `../NOTICE.md`.

PNG or JPG both work. Around 1200 px wide is enough — they are displayed at
roughly half the page width.

If permission is refused, delete this folder's images and leave the rest. The
tool's own rendering of the SEG-Y needs no permission and is interactive, which
is more useful than a scan in any case.
