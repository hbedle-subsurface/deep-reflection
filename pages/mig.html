Migration

On an unmigrated stack a dipping reflector is drawn shallower in dip and displaced updip, and a point scatterer is drawn as a hyperbola. Migration moves the energy back toward where it came from. This is a constant-velocity migration, so one velocity serves the whole record.

Dip in degrees

With "Drag to measure dip" chosen above the panels, a line dragged along a reflector on the input or the migrated panel is measured. The slope in time is turned into a dip with the average velocity of the model at that time.
