A file from disk

Any 2D poststack SEG-Y, in either byte order, format codes 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 8 through 12 and 16. Extended text headers are skipped. Dropping a file on the panel above does the same thing.

The file is read in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

What was readValueFrom

What this tool does with it

Each later step takes the section the step before it produced, does one thing to it, and shows what it kept beside what it removed. Amplitude recovery, then f-k separation on dip and frequency, then smoothing along structure, then spectral balancing. After that the section can be measured with attributes and classified. Steps can be skipped, and the attribute pages work on whichever stage is furthest along.

No migration, no velocity analysis, no prestack gathers, no 3D. Nothing here moves an event: where every reflection sits is whatever the original processing left.

What the crop decides

        
The f-k transform on the later steps sees only what is selected, so
          the dips and frequencies it measures are the ones inside the box. A
          crop that takes in the muted top of the record, or the part below
          which there is only noise, puts those into the measurement.

      

    


    
    

      
What is worth settling on this line before anything is
        applied to it.


      
Does the section match what the headers
        claim? A record length that disagrees with the panel, or a section
        that is half blank, usually means a field was filled in wrongly rather
        than that the file is damaged.


      
Where does the signal stop? Turn the display
        gain on and find the level below which there is only noise. That sets
        what the later steps can recover and what they would be
        inventing.


      
Has gain already been applied? A section
        whose deep half is as strong as its shallow half was gained before it
        was written, and that is now part of the file. The text header is where
        any record of it would be.


      
Is there geometry? Without coordinates in
        the trace headers the horizontal axis is a trace number, not a
        distance, and a dip in milliseconds per trace cannot be turned into an
        angle.


      
What is this line for? Imaging lower-crustal
        fabric, following one reflector, and comparing reflection strength with
        depth lead to different choices at the gain and filter steps, and the
        choices are not interchangeable.
