Structure-oriented smoothing

Each sample is averaged along the local reflector dip, measured with the structure tensor. Edge protection holds back the averaging where the section is not layered, which is where faults and terminations are.

How the settings are usually chosen

  1. The dip estimation window is set near half a period of the band center, so the measured dip follows the reflectors rather than single wavelet cycles.
  2. Edge protection stays near 2, so faults, terminations and chaotic zones are left largely unsmoothed.
  3. The smoothing length is raised a few traces at a time. Random noise leaves as speckle; once the removed panel starts to show layering that runs along the reflectors, the length has passed the point where reflections are being smoothed away, and the length just below it is kept.
  4. Strength below 1 blends the smoothed section with the input.

The checks under the panels measure each of these as the sliders move.
