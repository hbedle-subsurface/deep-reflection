Step 8 of 11

Attributes

An attribute is a number computed from the section at every sample, giving a second image the same size as the first. Four families are shown here, each measuring something different about the same traces.

Display

Each tab is one family of attributes. The panel above stays where it is.

An attribute is computed from the samples already in the section. Nothing is added: every attribute image is a rearrangement of what the recording and the processing left behind. What changes is which part of it is placed in front of the eye.

The four families answer different questions. Amplitude and energy attributes measure how much came back. Frequency attributes measure the scale of the features that returned it. Phase attributes discard amplitude entirely and show only where events are continuous. Geometry attributes measure the orientation of the fabric rather than its strength.

The panel above shows the amplitude section on the left and the attribute on the right, on the same axes. An attribute read on its own tends to be over-read. Read beside the amplitude it came from, the question becomes whether the attribute is marking something the amplitude section does not already show.

Attributes do not add information

A colorful attribute image computed from a noisy section is a colorful image of noise. If an event cannot be seen in the amplitude panel at all, an attribute derived from that panel is not evidence that it is there.

Try this

  1. Open the amplitude and energy tab and leave it on the amplitude volume transform, which is what the page starts with. Change the window length and watch the scale of the banding change with it.
  2. Switch to instantaneous phase in the phase tab. Every event now has the same strength. Compare the lower half of the section against the amplitude panel beside it.

Where this sits in the sequence

The attributes on this page are computed from the section produced by the earlier steps. The label beside the amplitude panel says which stage that is. An attribute computed before filtering and the same attribute computed after are different images, and the difference is the filtering rather than the geology.

A phase image always looks continuous

Dividing amplitude out means a sample with no reflection in it is displayed as strongly as one with a reflection. Phase images of the deep part of a record, where there may be little signal, still show banded continuity.

Attribute images are persuasive. Most of the ways they mislead are settled before the arithmetic runs, by what was computed from and with which window. These are the questions worth holding against whatever is on screen.

Which section did this come from? The label beside the amplitude panel says which stage. The same attribute computed before and after f-k filtering gives two different images, and the difference between them is the filter.

Is the feature visible in the amplitude panel? An attribute cannot recover an event that is not in the section it was computed from. Where the attribute marks something the amplitude panel shows nothing of, the first candidate is the estimator rather than the rock.

How does the window compare with the wavelet? A window shorter than one wavelet measures the wavelet. A window longer than the interval being examined averages across its boundaries. Moving the slider and watching the scale of the pattern change shows which regime the current setting is in.

Could the value be outside the band the file holds? An 8 ms sample interval puts the Nyquist frequency at 62.5 Hz. Frequency attributes reading well above that are reporting on interference between events, not on frequencies in the data.

Which way does the structure strike? Coherence and the other lateral measures compare traces along the profile. A fault striking with the line changes little from trace to trace and produces little response, whatever its displacement.

Is the display doing the work? Clip percentile, color map and vertical exaggeration all change how strong a pattern looks without changing a number. For relief shading the shadow fraction under the geometry controls is the specific check.

Are two of these the same measurement? Envelope, RMS amplitude and the amplitude volume transform are all built from the magnitude of the analytic signal and differ mainly in window length. Agreement between them is not confirmation.

What this page does not settle

How much of a chosen set of attributes is redundant, which is measured directly on the next page and matters before any of them are fed to a classification.

Whether a pattern is present in the earth or produced by the recording geometry. Two lines crossing the same structure, or a comparison against independent data, is what separates those.
