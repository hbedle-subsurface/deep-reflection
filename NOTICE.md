## Seismic data

**`data/COCORP_WY01_coherency.sgy`**

Consortium for Continental Reflection Profiling (COCORP), Wyoming Line 1,
coherency-filtered stack. Acquired 1976 across the southeastern Wind River
Mountains, Wyoming. Retrieved from the Cornell University ELLIPSE archive,
`cocorp.eas.cornell.edu/ELLIPSE/`.

783 traces, 2501 samples, 8 ms sample interval, 20 s record, SEG-Y format 1
(IBM float), big-endian. No geometry in the trace headers.

COCORP was a National Science Foundation funded academic programme based at
Cornell University. The ELLIPSE archive states no license or data-use terms.
This file is redistributed here for teaching. Anyone reusing it should cite
COCORP and the archive, and should contact Cornell for anything beyond
teaching use.
